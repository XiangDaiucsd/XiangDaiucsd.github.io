Research

Single-shot HDR using Conventional Image Sensor Shutter Functions and Optical Randomization

Paper
ACM Transactions on Graphics (TOG) Invited to present at SIGGRAPH 2026

Demonstrates a single-shot high dynamic range imaging system using conventional off-the-shelf optics combined with computational methods. The system encodes high dynamic range scenes into a single low-bit sensor measurement using optical randomization, then recovers the original image through regularized optimization, achieving dynamic ranges up to 73 dB from 8-bit measurements.

RnGCam: High-speed Video from Rolling & Global Shutter Measurements

Paper
ICCV Co-first author

Proposes a compressive high-speed video system (RnGCam) that fuses measurements from low-cost consumer-grade rolling-shutter and global-shutter cameras. A rolling-shutter sensor with a diffuser provides high temporal resolution while a conventional camera captures spatial details. Using implicit neural representations to fuse the complementary measurements, the system achieves 4,800 fps video at 10× lower cost than previous compressive video systems.

Quantitative Jones Matrix Imaging using Vectorial Fourier Ptychography

Paper
Biomedical Optics Express Editor's Pick

Extends Fourier ptychography to vector imaging using Jones calculus to fully characterize polarization effects. The method quantitatively maps optical material properties including retardance, diattenuation, and orientation with micrometer-scale spatial resolution. Experimental demonstrations achieved ~29 mm² field-of-view reconstructions at 1.24 µm resolution on polarization-sensitive samples.
