Research Experience

Particle Vertex Reconstruction in 3D

May 2024 – Mar 2026

UC San Diego, Lawrence Livermore National Lab · Advisor: Nick Antipa, Felicia Sutanto, Jingke Xu

  • Prototyped a multi-focal lens array enabling 3D particle vertex localization within a 5 cm³ volume.
  • Developed rendering-based forward models and Fisher Information analysis to establish theoretical system sensitivity.
  • Applied the ADCG algorithm for 3D particle reconstruction.

Single-shot HDR using Conventional Image Sensor Shutter Functions and Optical Randomization

Sep 2021 – May 2024

UC San Diego · Advisor: Nick Antipa

  • Designed and prototyped a single-shot HDR system using a customized random fiber bundle and off-the-shelf optics.
  • Developed FISTA with total variation regularization, achieving a dynamic range of up to 73 dB from an 8-bit input.

High-speed Video from Rolling & Global Shutter Measurements

Sep 2024 – Mar 2025

UC San Diego · Advisor: Nick Antipa

  • Proposed a dual-sensor hardware design combining a diffuser camera with a conventional camera for high-speed video.
  • Prototyped the dual-sensor, achieving 4,800 frames per second at 10× lower cost than existing systems.

Quantitative Jones Matrix Imaging using Vectorial Fourier Ptychography

Aug 2019 – Jun 2021

Duke University · Advisor: Roarke Horstmeyer

  • Extended scalar Fourier ptychography to a vectorial model based on Jones calculus to capture full polarization effects.
  • Demonstrated the method experimentally, achieving ~29 mm² field-of-view reconstructions at 1.24 µm resolution, quantitatively mapping retardance, diattenuation, and orientation.
